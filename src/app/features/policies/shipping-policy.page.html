1. Dispatch and Delivery Timeline

Orders are generally processed within 1 to 3 business days and delivered based on courier serviceability. Delivery timelines are estimates and may vary due to weather, operational constraints or courier delays.

2. Delivery Charges

Delivery charges are applicable as per order value, location and shipping method. Applicable charges are shown during checkout and must be paid by customer.

3. Address and Contact Accuracy

  • Customers are responsible for correct address, pin code and reachable phone number.

4. Risk and Liability After Delivery

Once courier marks shipment as delivered successfully, all risk transfers to the customer. Zaava Collection and business owner are not liable for any loss, theft, tampering, damage, delay claims or misuse after confirmed delivery.

5. Delivery Acceptance

Customer should inspect package condition at the time of receipt. Concerns about visible parcel tampering should be reported immediately with proof at delivery time.
